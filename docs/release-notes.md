## What's new

- **Opening check (Android).** When Instagram or another distracting app opens after 5 minutes away, by any route, one tap says what for: Message, Look something up or Browse. Quick purposes open at once with a time window grod learns from your own sessions; one that keeps running over loses its quick open; Browse waits first, longer with each Browse that day. It replaces the launcher's fixed pause.
- **A calmer grod app.** Four tabs (Today, Notifications, Apps, Settings) with Lucide icons.
- **Notification history on the phone.** Every notification with its full text, one line per chat message or email; search and an app filter; swipe a row to delete it, or delete a whole app, conversation, day or everything.
- **Notifications five times a day** by default: 08:30, 12:30, 15:30, 18:30, 21:30.
- **Wallpapers** for phone and desktop on the website.

## Install

**Android** (Android 8+)
1. Download `grod-<version>.apk` below and open it on the phone. Allow installing from your browser or file manager when asked.
2. Open grod and follow the checklist at the top: turn on the grod accessibility service, then notification access, Do Not Disturb access and usage access.
   - On Android 13 and newer, the accessibility switch is greyed out for sideloaded apps at first. Go to Settings → Apps → grod → ⋮ → **Allow restricted settings**, then try again.
3. Optional: in the app, set a rules URL (for example the raw link to your own `grod.rules.json`) so rule changes reach the phone without a cable.

**Browser** (Chrome, Comet, Arc, Brave, Edge)
1. Download `grod-extension-<version>.zip` and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder.

**macOS menu bar app, CLI and the AI-agent workflow:** clone the repository and follow the README.

Signed with the grod release key. Updates install over the previous version.
